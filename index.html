Our Heritage

Fifty Years of
Obsession

In a basement atelier beneath the old town of Genève, Henri Vacheron finished his first movement by lantern light. He had one rule: *a watch that isn't perfect isn't finished.*

Five decades later, that rule remains law. Every CHRONOS is assembled by a single master watchmaker, tested across 16 days and 1,200 hours of regulation, and signed by hand beneath the sapphire.

  • Swiss-made automatic calibres, 100% in-house
  • Each piece engraved with its unique edition number
  • Passed down — serviced for life, anywhere on Earth
